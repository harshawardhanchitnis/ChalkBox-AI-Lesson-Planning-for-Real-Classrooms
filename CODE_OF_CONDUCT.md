# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want ChalkBox to be a welcoming, professional, and constructive open-source project. Everyone participating in the project is expected to treat others with respect and to help maintain an environment where people can contribute safely.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive participation include:

- being respectful and considerate;
- giving specific, actionable technical feedback;
- welcoming contributors with different levels of experience;
- focusing disagreements on ideas and evidence rather than people;
- respecting privacy, classroom safety, accessibility, and copyright concerns;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory conduct;
- personal attacks, insults, or deliberately inflammatory comments;
- publishing someone else's private information without permission;
- sexualized content or unwanted sexual attention;
- spam, impersonation, coordinated abuse, or bad-faith disruption;
- knowingly submitting secrets, personal student information, or other sensitive data to the repository.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project-related community spaces, and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, or other contributions that violate these expectations. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

If you need to report conduct privately, contact the maintainer through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Please include enough context to investigate while avoiding unnecessary disclosure of personal information.

## Maintainer responsibility

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented where appropriate, and focused on protecting the community and the project rather than retaliating against criticism or disagreement.
